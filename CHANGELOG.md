# Bouncy Blobs changelog

Player-facing notes, one section per version, newest first. The nightly adds
tonight's section before it builds; `scripts/release-notes.sh` turns the newest
section into `VERSION.txt` and the Steam patch notes.

## 3.0.0

Bouncy Blobs 3.0 is a rebuild of the whole game: new engine, new story, new look.

**New in this build**
- **An opening cutscene.** The story now starts with Pip waking up in the dark:
  eyes open, the cave fades in and Pip stays framed the whole time.
- **Kids Mode has a real level.** A safe little room to bounce around in, a
  one-tap HOME button, and blobs that are easy to grab and drag without
  breaking.
- **Softer title screen.** The blobs on the title screen are squishy again: grab
  them by the skin and fling them. They fall gently, and fast ones are easier
  to catch.
- **Try it free.** The first two chapters are free to play. When you reach the
  end of the demo, a card shows you how to get the full game.
- **Buy and Wishlist.** Buy the full game from inside the game: on a computer,
  scan the QR code with your phone to check out, and the game unlocks by
  itself when the purchase goes through. Or tap Wishlist to add it on Steam.
- **Menu fixes.** Every screen has a way back, with a mouse, keyboard, gamepad
  or touch. The end-of-demo card fits every screen size, and the title menu
  feels smoother.

**The story**
- Ten chapters through a glowing cave, from *Too Bouncy* to *The Light Well*,
  with Glimmer as your guide and friends to rescue along the way.
- Gadgets to play with: launch gears, seed-pod launchers, balloons, a claw
  crane, mushrooms that bounce you sky-high, crushers, drips, ropes and vines.
- A living cave: glowing veins and mushrooms that light up as you pass,
  dripping goop, water, wind and parallax depth.

**Blobs**
- New squishy physics. Puff up to jump, squash down to dive, roll down ramps
  and cling to walls.
- Eat the little floating amoebas to take on their colour.
- Popping bursts into soft droplets that linger for a while.

**Party**
- Play with friends on one screen, or let everyone join from their phone by
  scanning the QR code: no app needed.
- King of the Hill on every map, plus Race and Chained Together.

**Also**
- Achievements, saved progress, and a streamer mode for the join panel.
