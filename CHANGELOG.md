# Bouncy Blobs changelog

Player-facing notes, one section per version, newest first. The nightly adds
tonight's section before it builds; `scripts/release-notes.sh` turns the newest
section into `VERSION.txt`, the Steam patch notes and the partii update page.
Lead each version's "### New" list with its biggest gameplay features: the
Discord post takes the first four.

## 3.0.0

Bouncy Blobs 3.0 is a rebuild of the whole game: new engine, new story, new look.

### New
- **A ten-chapter story.** Pip's journey through a glowing cave, from *Too
  Bouncy* to *The Light Well*, with Glimmer as your guide and friends to rescue
  along the way.
- **Party with phones.** Play with friends on one screen, or let everyone join
  from their phone by scanning the QR code: no app needed. King of the Hill on
  every map, plus Race and Chained Together.
- **Squishy new blob physics.** Puff up to jump, squash down to dive, roll down
  ramps and cling to walls. Eat floating amoebas to take on their colour.
- **Kids Mode.** A safe little room to bounce around in, with letters, music,
  shapes and colours, a one-tap HOME button, and friends that are easy to grab
  and drag.
- **Gadgets to play with.** Launch gears, seed-pod launchers, balloons, a claw
  crane, mushrooms that bounce you sky-high, crushers, drips, ropes and vines.
- **A living cave.** Glowing veins and mushrooms that light up as you pass,
  dripping goop, water, wind and parallax depth.
- **An opening cutscene.** The story starts with Pip waking up in the dark:
  eyes open, the cave fades in and Pip stays framed the whole time.
- **Try it free.** The first two chapters are free to play, and friends can
  always join your game for free.
- **Buy from inside the game.** On a computer, scan the QR code with your phone
  to check out, and the game unlocks by itself when the purchase goes through.
  Or tap Wishlist to add it on Steam.
- **Squishy title screen.** Grab the title-screen blobs by the skin and fling
  them.
- Achievements, saved progress, and a streamer mode that hides join codes.

### Fixes
- Every screen has a way back, with a mouse, keyboard, gamepad or touch.
- The end-of-demo card and speech bubbles fit every screen size.
- The title menu no longer jumbles its buttons, and Kids Mode always opens
  properly from the title.
