# Bouncy Blobs — Up & Out (Story Bible, 3.0)

> *Keep looking. Somebody out there is looking for you too.*

**Tone:** hope, joy, a little crazy. A softbody wakes up alone and goes
looking for a friend. Everyone he meets says no — grumpily, sillily, sadly —
and every "no" is funny and a little bit sweet, because Pip never stops
bouncing. Never grim. The world is soft: the ground jiggles, the walls
wobble, the text squishes.

## The World

Deep under a backyard is a cave. At the very bottom is a still, dark
puddle. One day, something in the puddle **wakes up**.

High above — so far it's barely a rumour — is a crack of daylight at the top
of an old well. Nobody down here goes up. Nobody down here goes anywhere.

## Characters

**Pip (the player).** A gumdrop-sized jelly who blinks into being at the
bottom of the cave. Curious, earnest, bouncy, and very much alone. Pip wants
exactly one thing: *a friend.* The player picks Pip's colour (and amoebas
he eats recolour him).

**Glimmer** (ch2). A glowworm who guards *her* patch of light and does not
share. "Shoo. You're making shadows."

**The Puffcaps** (ch3). A gang of mushroom folk who bounce strangers out of
the Puff Patch, giggling the whole time.

**The Tangles** (ch4). Vine critters who think every vine is theirs.
"Find your own dangly thing!"

**Clonk** (ch5). An ancient stone piston with a face. Says only "CLONK."
Means it.

**Reg — His Royal Softness, King Reginald Sock the First** (ch6). A lost sock
who crowned himself king of the underground lake. Pompous, rude, completely
wrong about everything — except one thing: up there, there is **a girl**.

**The Gusties** (ch7). Wind sprites who blow Pip around because it's funny.

**Button** (ch8). A one-eyed teddy bear at the bottom of the toy pile.
Tired and cynical — "Kids love you till they don't" — but it's Button who
points Pip at the crayon drawing: *LULU ♥ BOUNCY THINGS.*

**The Grumbles** (ch9). Grumpy little rock-blobs trapped by a cave-in. Pip
rescues every one of them anyway. "…Thanks. Now shoo."

**Lulu** (ch10). A little girl who lives in the house above the cave. She
loves bouncy things. She has been looking for a friend too.

## The Arc (10 chapters)

Each chapter is a long level (JellyCar length) with 3–5 lantern-flower
checkpoints, and an in-engine softbody cutscene between chapters. Each adds a
toy and remixes earlier ones.

| # | Chapter | Place | New mechanics | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Awake** | the Bottom Puddle | roll, expand-jump, crouch, jelly ground | Pip wakes up with no eyes; they draw themselves in like pen strokes, open and roll into place. Alone. "Hello?" Only echoes. A pinprick of light, impossibly far up. "Maybe somebody's up there." |
| 2 | **The Drip** | Drip Tunnels | falling drips, sagging bridges, membranes | Glimmer won't share her light. "Go glow somewhere else." Pip: "I can't glow." "Then go *not glow* somewhere else." |
| 3 | **Mushroom Bounce** | the Puff Patch | trampoline caps, springs, momentum | The Puffcaps bounce Pip out of their patch, giggling. He discovers bouncing is how you go UP. |
| 4 | **Vine Gardens** | Glowroot Grotto | vines: grab, swing, climb | The Tangles hog the vines. Pip finds his own. |
| 5 | **The Crusher Hall** | Old Stone Works | pistons, conveyors, lifts | Clonk. "Want to be friends?" "CLONK." The low point: popped again and again, Pip keeps reforming. "Still me. Still looking." |
| 6 | **Puddle Lake** | Underground Lake | water, floating, currents | King Reg won't let commoners near his teacup — but tells of the surface and the girl who lost him. Hope. |
| 7 | **Windy Chimneys** | Updraft Flues | updrafts, puff-to-float, fans, sticky walls | The Gusties blow Pip around for fun. From far above: a little girl's giggle. |
| 8 | **The Toy Pile** | Lost-Toy Midden | balls, see-saws, wind-up cars, slinkies | Button the teddy: "Kids love you till they don't." Then the drawing: LULU ♥ BOUNCY THINGS. "That's ME! I'm a bouncy thing!" |
| 9 | **The Cave-In** | Collapsed Hollow | rescue, crushers, everything | A rumble traps the Grumbles. They were never nice to anyone. Pip frees them all. "…Thanks. Now shoo." Pip smiles anyway. |
| 10 | **The Light Well** | The Old Well | a long vertical climb, everything | Alone, upward, into the light. Two enormous gentle hands. Lulu: "You're SO SQUISHY! Do you want to come home with me?" Pip: "...Yes." Credits roll in her playroom. |

## Cutscene language

* **Cinematic, in-engine softbody puppetry.** Slow fades, letterbox, push-ins
  and reveals; actors are real softbodies (roll, hop, puff, squash, wobble).
* **One voice at a time.** A new line closes any bubble still on screen.
* **Faces act:** eyes draw themselves in and roll into place on first waking, gaze, moods.
* **Pip stays in frame.** The cutscene camera always fits Pip (plus a margin
  and the letterbox): looking up at something far away or jumping, it zooms
  out rather than lose Pip. Only a deliberate close-up (`closeup`) leaves Pip out.
* **Every bubble is fully on screen** (inside the letterbox, at any aspect).
* Speech bubbles are jelly, tethered to the speaker; gibberish "blub" voices.
* Every cutscene is skippable (hold) and under ~60 s.

## Rules of the world

1. Nothing is hard. Ground, walls, bridges all wobble.
2. Popping isn't the end — you splat and reform at the last lantern.
3. Being crushed pops only *you*. Bystanders get a "phew!".
4. Every "no" is funny. Pip never stops looking.
